# False Doctrine 1: Papal Titles and the Claim to Divine Authority
## A Bible-Only Study

---

## Introduction: Why This Matters

The Bible warns repeatedly of a religious power that would arise, speak "pompous words against the Most High," claim divine prerogatives, and change God's law (Daniel 7:25). This is not a vague prophecy—the titles, claims, and documented actions of the papal system provide a precise historical match. This study examines those claims against Scripture alone, allowing God's Word to be the judge.

> **Isaiah 8:20** — "To the law and to the testimony! If they do not speak according to this word, it is because there is no light in them."

---

## Part 1: The Official Papal Titles

The papacy holds the following titles, documented in the *Annuario Pontificio* (Pontifical Yearbook) and confirmed by Catholic canon law:

1. **Bishop of Rome**
2. **Vicar of Jesus Christ** (*Vicarius Christi*)
3. **Successor of the Prince of the Apostles**
4. **Supreme Pontiff of the Universal Church** (*Summus Pontifex*)
5. **Pontifex Maximus** — originally the title of pagan Roman high priests
6. **Holy Father**
7. **His Holiness**
8. **Vicar of the Son of God** (*Vicarius Filii Dei*) — historical title
9. **Servant of the Servants of God**

Each title is examined below against the standard of Scripture.

---

## Part 2: What the Bible Says About These Titles

### Title 1: "Pontifex Maximus" — Supreme High Priest and Mediator Between God and Man

**Historical Note**: This title belonged to the pagan high priests of Rome. It was held by Roman emperors (Julius Caesar held it from 63 BCE). It passed to the Bishop of Rome as the empire's religious power faded.

**What Scripture Says About the Only True High Priest**:

> "Therefore, holy brethren, partakers of the heavenly calling, consider the Apostle and **High Priest of our confession, Christ Jesus**." (Hebrews 3:1)

> "Seeing then that we have a **great High Priest** who has passed through the heavens, Jesus the Son of God, let us hold fast our confession." (Hebrews 4:14)

> "For there is **one God and one Mediator between God and men, the Man Christ Jesus**." (1 Timothy 2:5)

> "Therefore He is also able to save to the uttermost those who come to God through Him, since **He always lives to make intercession for them**." (Hebrews 7:25)

**Conclusion**: Scripture assigns the role of Supreme High Priest and sole Mediator exclusively and permanently to Jesus Christ. No human being can hold this role. Claiming to be the "Supreme Pontiff" (supreme bridge-builder / highest priest) usurps what belongs to Christ alone.

---

### Title 2: "Vicar of Christ / Vicar of the Son of God" — Representative of Christ on Earth

**Meaning**: *Vicar* = substitute; one who acts *in place of* another.

**What Scripture Says About Christ's Appointed Vicar**:

Jesus explicitly named His personal representative after His ascension:

> "And I will pray the Father, and He will give you **another Helper**, that He may abide with you forever — **the Spirit of truth**." (John 14:16-17)

> "But the **Helper, the Holy Spirit**, whom the Father will send in My name, He will teach you all things, and bring to your remembrance all things that I said to you." (John 14:26)

> "However, when He, the **Spirit of truth**, has come, He will guide you into all truth." (John 16:13)

**Conclusion**: Jesus designated the Holy Spirit — not a human religious leader — as His vicar and representative. Claiming the title "Vicar of Christ" directly usurps the role God assigned to the Holy Spirit.

---

### Title 3: "Holy Father" — Common Papal Address

**What Scripture Says**:

Jesus addressed "Holy Father" exclusively to God:

> "**Holy Father**, keep through Your name those whom You have given Me, that they may be one as We are." (John 17:11)

Jesus explicitly forbade applying this title to any human religious leader:

> "**Do not call anyone on earth your father; for One is your Father, He who is in heaven.**" (Matthew 23:9)

**Conclusion**: "Holy Father" is a name Jesus reserved for God alone. Scripture explicitly prohibits applying it to earthly religious figures.

---

### Title 4: "Supreme Pontiff of the Universal Church" — Head over All Christians

**What Scripture Says About the Head of the Church**:

> "And He is the **head of the body, the church**, who is the beginning, the firstborn from the dead, that in all things He may have the preeminence." (Colossians 1:18)

> "And He put all things under His feet, and gave Him to be **head over all things to the church**, which is His body, the fullness of Him who fills all in all." (Ephesians 1:22-23)

> "For the husband is head of the wife, as also **Christ is head of the church**; and He is the Savior of the body." (Ephesians 5:23)

**Conclusion**: Christ—and Christ alone—is the head of the universal Church. This position cannot be delegated to or assumed by any human being.

## Part 3: Biblical Definition of Blasphemy

Before applying biblical standards, we must define what the Bible itself calls blasphemy.

### Blasphemy Type 1: Claiming to Be God or Equal with God

> "When Jesus saw their faith, He said to the paralytic, 'Son, your sins are forgiven you.' And some of the scribes... reasoning in their hearts, **'Why does this Man speak blasphemies like this? Who can forgive sins but God alone?'**" (Mark 2:5-7)

> "Then the Jews took up stones again to stone Him... saying, '**For a good work we do not stone You, but for blasphemy, and because You, being a Man, make Yourself God.**'" (John 10:33)

### Blasphemy Type 2: Usurping God's Name or Titles

> "**My glory I will not give to another.**" (Isaiah 42:8)

> "And whoever **blasphemes the name of the LORD** shall surely be put to death." (Leviticus 24:16)

### Blasphemy Type 3: Claiming Divine Prerogatives (Changing God's Law, Speaking Against God)

> "He shall **speak pompous words against the Most High**, shall persecute the saints of the Most High, and shall intend to **change times and law**." (Daniel 7:25)

> "The man of sin is revealed... who opposes and **exalts himself above all that is called God** or that is worshiped, so that he sits as God in the temple of God, showing himself that he is God." (2 Thessalonians 2:3-4)

## Part 4: Applying Biblical Standards to Papal Claims

### Claim 1: Priests Can Forgive Sins

**Catholic Catechism (CCC 1461)**: The Church teaches that bishops and priests have the "ministry of reconciliation"—the ability to absolve sins through the sacrament of confession.

**What Scripture Says**:

> "Why does this Man speak blasphemies like this? **Who can forgive sins but God alone?**" (Mark 2:7)

> **Acts 5:31** — "Him God has exalted... to give repentance to Israel and **forgiveness of sins**." [God alone gives it]

> **1 John 1:9** — "If we confess our sins, **He [God] is faithful and just to forgive us our sins**."

**Conclusion**: Scripture establishes forgiveness of sins as an exclusively divine act. Claiming institutional power to forgive sins is biblical blasphemy.

---

### Claim 2: Papal Infallibility

**Catholic Doctrine (Vatican I, 1870)**: When the Pope speaks *ex cathedra* on matters of faith and morals, he is preserved from error by divine protection.

**What Scripture Says**:

> "For **all have sinned** and fall short of the glory of God." (Romans 3:23)

> "If we say that we have no sin, **we deceive ourselves**, and the truth is not in us." (1 John 1:8)

> "There is **none righteous, no, not one.**" (Romans 3:10)

> "God alone has immortality, dwelling in **unapproachable light**." (1 Timothy 6:16)

**Conclusion**: Infallibility is an attribute that belongs to God alone. Any human claiming infallibility is claiming a divine attribute, which Scripture expressly denies to all people.

---

### Claim 3: Authority to Change God's Ten Commandments

**Historical Record**: The Catholic Church removed the 2nd Commandment (graven images), altered the 4th Commandment (Sabbath → Sunday), and split the 10th Commandment to restore the count of ten.

**Catholic Admission**:
> "The Church changed the observance of the Sabbath to Sunday by right of the divine, infallible authority given to her by her Founder, Jesus Christ." (*Catholic Universe Bulletin*, August 14, 1942)

**What Scripture Says**:

> "**You shall not add to the word which I command you, nor take from it**, that you may keep the commandments of the LORD your God which I command you." (Deuteronomy 4:2)

> "For assuredly, I say to you, **till heaven and earth pass away, one jot or one tittle will by no means pass from the law** till all is fulfilled." (Matthew 5:18)

> "Do not think that I have come to abolish the Law or the Prophets; **I have not come to abolish them but to fulfill them.**" (Matthew 5:17)

> "He shall speak pompous words against the Most High... and shall intend to **change times and law**." (Daniel 7:25)

**Conclusion**: Claiming the authority to alter God's Ten Commandments is the clearest possible example of exalting oneself above God's Word—prophetically described in Daniel 7:25.

## Part 5: Prophetic Fulfillment — Three Key Passages

### Daniel 7:25

> "He shall speak **pompous words against the Most High**, shall **persecute the saints** of the Most High, and shall intend to **change times and law**. Then the saints shall be given into his hand for a time and times and half a time."

**Three-Part Test Applied to the Papal System**:

| **Prophetic Mark** | **Historical Fulfillment** |
|-------------------|---------------------------|
| Speak pompous words against the Most High | Claims infallibility, title "Holy Father," "Vicar of God" |
| Persecute the saints | Inquisition, persecution of Sabbath-keepers, Waldenses, Reformers |
| Change times and law | Changed Sabbath from 7th to 1st day; altered Ten Commandments |

---

### 2 Thessalonians 2:3-4

> "Let no one deceive you by any means; for that Day will not come unless the falling away comes first, and **the man of sin is revealed, the son of perdition, who opposes and exalts himself above all that is called God** or that is worshiped, so that **he sits as God in the temple of God, showing himself that he is God**."

**Three Marks Applied**:

| **Prophetic Mark** | **Application** |
|-------------------|----|
| Opposes and exalts himself above God | Claims authority to change God's commandments |
| Sits as God in the temple of God | Claims to be head of the Church (Christ's body/temple) |
| Shows himself that he is God | Claims infallibility, divine titles, power to forgive sins |

---

### Revelation 13:5-6

> "And he was given a mouth **speaking great things and blasphemies**... Then he opened his mouth in **blasphemy against God, to blaspheme His name, His tabernacle, and those who dwell in heaven**."

**Three marks of blasphemy applied**:
- **Blaspheme God's name**: Claiming titles that belong to God ("Holy Father," "Lord God the Pope")
- **Blaspheme His tabernacle**: Claiming authority over the Church (God's temple — 1 Corinthians 3:16)
- **Blaspheme those who dwell in heaven**: Doctrines of purgatory and indulgences that imply Christ's sacrifice was insufficient

## Part 7: The Biblical Warning Against Human Tradition

Jesus addressed this pattern directly:

> "Well did Isaiah prophesy of you hypocrites, as it is written: 'This people honors Me with their lips, but their heart is far from Me. **And in vain they worship Me, teaching as doctrines the commandments of men**.' For **laying aside the commandment of God, you hold the tradition of men**." (Mark 7:6-8)

> "**All too well you reject the commandment of God**, that you may keep your tradition." (Mark 7:9)

God's warning is clear—when human religious authority substitutes its commandments for God's, worship becomes vain, regardless of sincerity.

---

## Part 8: God's Call to Individuals Within the System

It is essential to distinguish between **the system** (which Scripture identifies) and **the sincere people** within it. God makes this distinction clearly:

> "And I heard another voice from heaven saying, **'Come out of her, my people**, lest you share in her sins, and lest you receive of her plagues.'" (Revelation 18:4)

God calls them **"My people"** — sincere believers who have not yet seen these truths. The call is not to condemn individuals but to expose the system and call hearts back to Scripture alone.

## Conclusion

The biblical standard is clear. Every title and claim examined in this study places human authority in the place that Scripture reserves exclusively for God. This is not an attack on individuals—countless sincere Catholics love God deeply. It is an exposure of **the system** that prophetic Scripture foresaw and warned against.

The question every sincere reader must answer:

> **Will I follow what God has plainly said in His Word, or what a human institution has declared?**

> "We ought to **obey God rather than men**." (Acts 5:29)
