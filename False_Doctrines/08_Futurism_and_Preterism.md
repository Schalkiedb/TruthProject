## Part 1: The Three Prophetic Interpretation Methods Defined

### 1.1 Historicism — The Biblical and Reformation View

**What it teaches**: Bible prophecy describes the continuous flow of history from the prophet's time to the end of the world. Prophecy is fulfilled progressively throughout history in identifiable empires, religious powers, and events.

**Key characteristics**:
- The "day-year principle": prophetic days = literal years (Numbers 14:34; Ezekiel 4:6)
- Daniel's four kingdoms = Babylon, Medo-Persia, Greece, Rome — fulfilled historically
- The 1,260 days of Daniel 7:25 and Revelation 13:5 = 1,260 literal years (538–1798 CE)
- The "little horn" of Daniel 7 and the "beast" of Revelation 13 = the papal system historically
- Held by virtually all Protestant Reformers

---

### 1.2 Futurism — Invented by Jesuit Francisco Ribera (1590 CE)

**Origin**: **Francisco Ribera** (1537–1591), a Jesuit priest from Salamanca, Spain, published a 500-page commentary on the Book of Revelation in 1590. He proposed:
- Only the first few chapters of Revelation apply to pagan Rome
- The remaining chapters (most of the book) are reserved for a **future** 3½-year period at the very end of history
- A future individual Antichrist will arise, rebuild Jerusalem's temple, deny Christ, claim to be God, and abolish Christianity — all in 3½ literal years
- This shifts the Antichrist from a historical institution (Rome/papacy) to a single future individual

**Purpose**: By placing the Antichrist in a far future time, Rome was completely removed from the prophetic picture.

**How it entered Protestantism**:
- Dr. Samuel Maitland (1826 CE) — Anglican, discovered Ribera's work and published it
- John Nelson Darby (1830s CE) — incorporated Futurism into his dispensational system (see File 07)
- C.I. Scofield (1909 CE) — embedded it in the Scofield Reference Bible
- Today: The dominant system in evangelical Christianity

**What Futurism requires you to believe**:
- The "Antichrist" is NOT the papacy — it is a single future Jewish or secular individual
- The 1,260 days are literal days (3½ years), not prophetic years
- A future 7-year Tribulation follows the Rapture
- The "daily sacrifice" in Daniel requires a rebuilt Jerusalem temple
- The "abomination of desolation" is still future — not the papal mass replacing Christ's mediatorial role

---

### 1.3 Preterism — Invented by Jesuit Luis de Alcasar (~1614 CE)

**Origin**: **Luis de Alcasar** (1554–1613), another Jesuit priest, published *Vestigatio arcani sensus in Apocalypsi* (~1614 CE, posthumously). He proposed:
- Almost all of Revelation was already fulfilled in the first few centuries CE
- The "beast" = pagan Rome (Nero)
- The "harlot Babylon" = Jerusalem, destroyed in 70 CE
- Prophecy is essentially *finished* — we are already in the millennial/post-fulfillment era

**Purpose**: Same as Futurism — by placing Antichrist in the past (Nero/pagan Rome), the papacy is again shielded from prophetic identification.

**Varieties**:
- **Partial Preterism**: Most prophecy was fulfilled in 70 CE; the Second Coming and resurrection are still future
- **Full/Hyper Preterism**: ALL prophecy — including the Second Coming and resurrection — was fulfilled in 70 CE (considered heretical by most Christians)

## Part 2: Testing the Systems Against Scripture

### 2.1 The Day-Year Principle — Does the Bible Support It?

> "According to the number of the days in which you spied out the land, forty days, **for each day you shall bear your guilt one year**, namely forty years." (Numbers 14:34)

> "Lie also on your left side, and lay the iniquity of the house of Israel upon it... **I have laid on you a day for each year.**" (Ezekiel 4:4-6)

God Himself establishes the prophetic pattern: **one prophetic day = one literal year**. This principle, applied consistently, makes Historicism work.

**Futurism's problem**: If the 1,260 "days" of Daniel 7:25 and Revelation 13:5 are literal days (3½ years), the day-year principle God established is discarded in favor of a literal reading. Yet no persecution or reign of an Antichrist in a literal 3½-year period has ever occurred or fits any known fulfillment.

### 2.2 The 1,260 Days — A Historical Fulfillment

> "Then the saints shall be given into his hand for **a time and times and half a time**." (Daniel 7:25)

> "And he was given a mouth speaking great things and blasphemies, and he was given authority to continue for **forty-two months**." (Revelation 13:5)

> "...and they will tread the holy city underfoot for **forty-two months**..." (Revelation 11:2)

> "...she has a place prepared by God, that they should feed her there **one thousand two hundred and sixty days**." (Revelation 12:6)

All four expressions describe the SAME period:
- "Time, times, and half a time" = 1 + 2 + ½ = 3½ years
- 42 months = 3½ years
- 1,260 days = 42 months × 30 days = 3½ years

**Applying the day-year principle**: 1,260 prophetic days = **1,260 literal years**.

**Historical fulfillment**: **538 CE** (the year the last of the three Arian kingdoms opposing Rome fell, establishing papal political supremacy) to **1798 CE** (when General Berthier of Napoleon's forces entered Rome and took Pope Pius VI captive — the "deadly wound" of Revelation 13:3).

1798 – 538 = **1,260 years** — an exact fulfillment.

No Futurist 3½-year tribulation has ever produced this precision. The historical fulfillment is exact.

### 2.3 The "Abomination of Desolation" — Was It 70 CE or Still Future?

> "Therefore when you see the 'abomination of desolation,' spoken of by Daniel the prophet, standing in the holy place (whoever reads, let him understand), then let those who are in Judea flee to the mountains." (Matthew 24:15-16)

**Preterist claim**: Fulfilled in 70 CE when Roman armies surrounded Jerusalem.

**Problem**: Daniel's vision in chapter 11-12 describes events across multiple kingdoms and centuries — not just one event in 70 CE. The "little horn" of Daniel 7 goes far beyond Roman armies and encompasses centuries of law-changing and saint-persecution.

**Futurist claim**: A rebuilt Jerusalem temple awaits, where a future Antichrist will place an idol.

**Problem**: The New Testament teaches that believers — not a stone building — are God's temple:

> "Do you not know that you are the temple of God and that the Spirit of God dwells in you?" (1 Corinthians 3:16)

> "...in whom you also are being built together for a dwelling place of God in the Spirit." (Ephesians 2:22)

The "holy place" that was desecrated is the spiritual temple — the church — when false doctrine and a false priesthood replaced Christ's mediatorial work. A rebuilt stone temple is not "the holy place" in New Covenant theology.

## Part 3: The Reformers' Unanimous Position

The identification of the papal system as the prophetic Antichrist/little horn/beast was not a minority fringe view. It was the consensus of the Reformation:

- **Martin Luther**: *"We here are of the conviction that the papacy is the seat of the true and real Antichrist."*

- **John Knox**: "That tyranny which the pope himself has for so many ages exercised over the church" — identified the papacy as Antichrist
- **William Tyndale**: "...the pope is the antichrist, and his doctrine sprung of the devil"
- **John Wesley**: "He is in an emphatical sense, the Man of Sin, as he increases all manner of sin above measure. And he is, too, properly styled the Son of Perdition"

This was not Protestant anti-Catholic bias. It was the consistent, independent conclusion from reading the Bible's own prophetic criteria and applying them to history.
