# Bible Symbols & Their Scriptural Meaning

> *"All Scripture is God-breathed and profitable for doctrine..."* — 2 Timothy 3:16

**A Comprehensive A–Z Reference Chart of Biblical Symbols, Their God-Intended Meaning, and Supporting Scripture**

---

> *"To the law and to the testimony! If they do not speak according to this word, it is because there is no light in them."* — Isaiah 8:20

> *"...comparing spiritual things with spiritual."* — 1 Corinthians 2:13

This chart presents biblical symbols as interpreted by the Bible itself. Each symbol's meaning is established solely from Scripture — God's Word explains God's symbols. Symbols are arranged alphabetically. All references are drawn from the Old and New Testaments.
