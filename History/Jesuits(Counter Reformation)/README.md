# Jesuits & The Counter-Reformation

> *"If there be no certainty in religion, we must not seek it in the Jesuits."*  
> — Blaise Pascal, Provincial Letters (1656)

## Purpose

This section covers the history of the **Society of Jesus (Jesuits)** and their role in the **Counter-Reformation** — the Roman Catholic Church's strategic response to the Protestant Reformation.

## Planned Content

### Core Documents
1. **The Founding of the Jesuits (1534–1540)** — Ignatius of Loyola, the Spiritual Exercises, and the establishment of the Order
2. **The Jesuit Oath** — Historical analysis of the controversial Fourth Vow and obedience to the Pope
3. **Counter-Reformation Strategies** — The Council of Trent (1545–1563), the Index of Forbidden Books, and the Inquisition
4. **Futurism & Preterism: Jesuit Prophetic Counter-Systems** — Francisco Ribera's futurism and Luis de Alcázar's preterism — two systems designed to deflect the Reformers' identification of the papacy as the Antichrist
5. **Jesuit Influence in Education** — The Ratio Studiorum and the reshaping of European intellectual life
6. **Jesuit Political Influence** — Expulsions from 83+ countries, involvement in political affairs, and the "Black Pope"
7. **The Jesuit Restoration (1814)** — Suppression by Pope Clement XIV (1773) and restoration by Pius VII

### Primary Sources
- Decrees of the Council of Trent
- Ignatius of Loyola's *Spiritual Exercises*
- Jesuit Relations (missionary reports)
- Papal bulls related to Jesuit activities
