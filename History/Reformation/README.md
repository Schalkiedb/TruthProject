# The Protestant Reformation

> *"Here I stand, I can do no other. God help me. Amen."*  
> — Martin Luther, Diet of Worms (1521)

## Purpose

This section covers the history of the **Protestant Reformation** — the movement that restored the Bible to the common people and challenged the authority of Rome with **Sola Scriptura** (Scripture Alone).

## Planned Content

### Core Documents
1. **Pre-Reformation Witnesses** — The Waldensians, John Wycliffe, Jan Hus, and the Lollards — those who preserved biblical truth before Luther
2. **Martin Luther and the 95 Theses (1517)** — The spark that ignited the Reformation, Luther's stand against indulgences, and his rediscovery of justification by faith
3. **Ulrich Zwingli and the Swiss Reformation** — The Reformed movement in Zürich and its influence across Europe
4. **John Calvin and Reformed Theology** — Institutes of the Christian Religion, predestination, and the Geneva experiment
5. **The English Reformation** — Henry VIII, Thomas Cranmer, the Tyndale Bible, and the establishment of the Church of England
6. **The Anabaptists and Radical Reformation** — Those who went further than the mainstream Reformers — believer's baptism, separation of church and state
7. **The Sabbath and the Reformation** — What the Reformers said about Saturday vs. Sunday, and why most stopped short of restoring the seventh-day Sabbath
8. **The Reformation's Unfinished Work** — How the Reformers identified the papacy as the Antichrist but retained many Roman doctrines and traditions
