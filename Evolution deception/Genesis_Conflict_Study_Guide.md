## Why This Matters

The first angel's message in Revelation 14:7 calls humanity to **"worship Him who made the heavens, the earth, the sea, and the springs of water."** This is a direct quotation of the fourth commandment — the Sabbath commandment — which is grounded in **Creation**.

If there is no Creator, there is no Sabbath. If there is no Sabbath, there is no seal of God. Evolution strikes at the very foundation of worship and loyalty that the final conflict is about.

> *"For in six days the Lord made the heavens and the earth, the sea, and all that is in them, and rested the seventh day."* — Exodus 20:11

Prof. Walter Veith — a former evolutionist and professor of zoology — presents the scientific evidence that dismantles the evolutionary framework and confirms the biblical account of origins.
